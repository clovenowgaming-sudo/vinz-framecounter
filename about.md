## Frame Window Counter
Auto frame window counter with its own macro recorder.

- Pause menu > small **FC** button (bottom right) opens the counter UI.
- **Record**: restarts the level and records your inputs. The completed run (or **Stop**) becomes the macro.
- **Import / Export**: `.gdr2` replays (GDR2 format). Put files in Download or the mod's `macros` folder.
- **Count**: replays the macro and finds how many ticks early/late each click can be.
